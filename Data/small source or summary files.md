## Small Source or Summary Files

| **File** | **Format** | **Purpose** | **Source** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Genome sequence** | FASTA (.fasta) | Contains the complete *Plumeria rubra* cultivar *Acutifolia* chloroplast genome sequence used for upload to Galaxy and sequence analysis. | NCBI Nucleotide, **MN812495.1** |
| **Annotated genome** | GenBank (.gb / .gbk) | Contains annotated genes, gene coordinates, CDS, tRNA, rRNA, introns, and other chloroplast genome features. | NCBI Nucleotide, **MN812495.1** |
| **Genome summary** | TXT / Markdown (.txt / .md) | Summarizes the organism, accession number, genome size, GC content, genome regions, and gene content. | **NCBI + Galaxy** |
| **Source information** | TXT / Markdown (.txt / .md) | Records the database, accession number, publication, and other sources used for the analysis. | **NCBI Nucleotide + published study** |
| **Galaxy analysis** | Galaxy History | Contains the workflow and results used to obtain sequence statistics, including genome length, sequence count, and GC content. | **UseGalaxy.org** |
